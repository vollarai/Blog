# Reading & Commenting

Browse articles and join the discussion. Reading is open to everyone. Commenting requires an account.

## Browsing posts

The home page shows the latest posts, newest first, three per page. Each card shows the author, publish date, a preview of the content, and a cover image if the post has one. Use the page numbers at the bottom of the list to move between pages. Click `Read more` on a post to open the full article.

## Reading a post

The article page shows the full title, author, publish date, cover image, and content.

> You don't need to be logged in to read posts or view existing comments.

## Leaving a comment

Scroll to the `Comments` section below the article.

> If you're not logged in, you'll see a prompt to [log in](/guide/getting_started#logging-in) - comments are only open to registered users.

Type your comment in the text box and select `Post comment`. Your comment appears at the bottom of the list right away, along with your name and the date.
