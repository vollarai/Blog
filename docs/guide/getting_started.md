# Getting Started

A platform for publishing original articles.  A place to read and discuss articles.

## Creating an account

- Go to Sign Up page and enter your data

- Check your inbox for a confirmation email and follow the link. Your account isn't fully active until the email is confirmed.

- If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check spam, or request it again from the sign-up/login screen.

## Log in

Go to `Login` page.

<video src="/log.mov" autoplay muted loop playsinline></video>

Use your confirmed email and password on the Login page. 
> If you enter the
wrong password too many times in a short window, login is temporarily
locked - wait a few minutes and try again.
